Open Hunt Data

Your hunting data, portable for life.

Open Hunt Data (OHD) is a vendor-neutral file format for a hunter's own records: harvests, sightings, hunts, stands and cameras, property boundaries, tracks, and the photos that go with them. Any app can export an OHD bundle. Any app can import one. Decades of seasons stop being locked inside whichever app happened to capture them.

Why

A hunter's records outlive any product. Apps get acquired, sunset, or simply stop fitting how someone hunts. When that happens, the data should leave with the person, in a form the next app can read without a support ticket. OHD is that form, published as an open standard so no single company owns it.

What a bundle contains

season-2025.ohd
├── manifest.json        version, exporting app, hashes, and what was left out and why
├── harvests.json        harvests with measurements, scores, weather
├── sightings.json       sightings with per-animal observations
├── hunts.json           sessions: start, end, stand, outcome, forecast vs actual
├── waypoints.geojson    stands, cameras, sign, access (GeoJSON Points)
├── areas.geojson        boundaries, food plots (GeoJSON Polygons)
├── tracks.geojson       recorded walks, drawn lines (GeoJSON LineStrings)
├── properties.json      named land with acreage and lease dates
├── media.json           index of photos and who they belong to
└── media/               the photos, named by content hash

A bundle is a zip or a plain folder. Map files are ordinary GeoJSON and open in QGIS or geojson.io as they are. Every record carries a stable id, the app it came from, and an extensions slot so apps can round-trip their own fields without polluting the core.

A bundle contains the subject's own authored records only. It never contains other people's names, contact details, reservation calendars, or records other users authored on shared maps. Exporters must offer a location-stripping option and must record its use in the manifest. (SPEC.md §6)

Conformance

LevelMeaning
Produces a bundle that passes ohd validate with zero errors, with a truthful coverage block, and lets the user export from inside the app.
Accepts any valid 1.x bundle without rejecting unknown fields, extensions, or dangling references, and reports what it imported and skipped.
Both, and re-exporting an imported bundle preserves every field and extension, verified by ohd roundtrip.
